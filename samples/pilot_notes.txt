Order intake automation: which plant pilots first?
Today orders arrive by email and PDF and are typed into the ERP by hand.
Manual entry takes 12 minutes per order on average.
Goal of the pilot: read orders automatically and create draft orders in the ERP.
Three candidate plants: North, South and East.
Plant North: 3,100 orders a year, 4% entry errors, IT support on site.
Plant South: 2,400 orders a year, 6% entry errors, no IT support on site.
Plant East: 1,800 orders a year, 3% entry errors, IT support on site.
Plant North has the most standard order formats: 70% of its orders use one template.
Plant South has the most scanned PDFs, which are harder to read.
Plant East is planning an ERP upgrade in spring 2027.
Pilot length 8 weeks, budget EUR 40k including licences and setup.
Success criteria: 80% of orders read without correction, entry time under 3 minutes.
A sales person still checks and approves every draft order during the pilot.
Risk: order formats change when customers update their systems.
Risk: the works council must be informed before the pilot starts.
Go-live target for the first plant: March 2027.
Recommendation: start in Plant North, highest volume, standard formats, IT on site.
Plant South second, after the reading of scanned PDFs is improved.
Decision by 30 Nov so the pilot can start in January.
